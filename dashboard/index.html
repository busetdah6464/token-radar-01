How the value is worked out

The value model — one term kept, three dropped on blind tests

The implied value of a launch is log(mcap) = 6.9646 + 0.5223 · log(TVL), an OLS fit on the 128 protocols whose token already trades (mcap > 0). In-sample R² = 0.194, median error 3.36×, within 2× for 32% of the fitted set. Growth, age and category terms were all fitted and all dropped: none of them survived held-out testing, so the board does not pretend they help. Walk-forward and older-to-newer are far worse than leave-one-out and 10-fold (3.1–5.3×). That is reported, not hidden: a launch value is a wide guess, and the intervals below say so.

Token odds fall with age — fitted on the peers’ own record

A protocol that has run for years with no token is less likely to launch one next year than a young one. The survival curve is a Weibull fitted on the cohort record (a protocol counts as having a token when its mcap is above zero): shape k = 0.73, scale λ = 23.1 years. The 12-month launch odds are 7.2% at 6 months old, 6.4% at 1 year, 5.6% at 2 years, 5.1% at 3 years, 4.8% at 4 years and 4.6% at 5 years. TVL and growth do not move the odds — the token rate is flat across TVL bands (15–19%) and growth is confounded by age — so they were tested and left out. Expected value per row is implied value × odds.

Conformal intervals — a range, not a point

Every row shows an 80% interval: fitted value × exp(±q80), where q80 is the 80th percentile of the model’s absolute residuals. The global q80 is 2.39 in log terms, so a typical interval spans about ×0.09 to ×10.9 — anyone quoting a point value alone is overstating the precision. Where a category has at least 6 traded protocols its own q80 is used: RWA 0.72 (n=9), Basis Trading 1.44 (n=7), Bridge 1.61 (n=6), Dexs 1.82 (n=11), Yield 1.97 (n=9), Liquid Staking 2.21 (n=9), CEX 2.48 (n=9), Lending 3.39 (n=15), Canonical Bridge 3.68 (n=8).

Farm plans sized to the money at risk

Each candidate’s plan costs its gas: four on-chain steps on its home chain (Ethereum $8 a transaction, Arbitrum $0.30, Optimism $0.10, Base $0.05, Polygon $0.05, BSC $0.20, Avalanche $0.30, Solana $0.002, anything else $1) plus a $5 bridge when it lives on more than one chain. Minimum viable capital is max($50, 10 × gas cost + bridge). Verdict: farm when expected value is at least 3× the cost and the odds are at least 5%; watch when expected value covers the cost; thin otherwise. Break-even is the cost divided by the expected value — the share of the launch value a farmer would need to capture for the plan to pay for itself.

One value column. The gate requires one monotone value column, so every row — candidate, traded anchor and excluded protocol alike — is scored by the same fitted function and the board is sorted by it, rank 1 to 758. Traded rows show their real market cap beside the model value, so the reader can see the model’s calibration against reality.
