Method — what was kept, what was dropped, and why

Fit set (restored). The value model is fitted only on traded protocols with TVL ≥ $5M and market-cap/TVL in [0.01, 10] — 111 protocols. The 17 dropped by the filter are extreme outliers: bridges and staked products with tiny market caps against huge TVL (SSV Network $10.2B TVL / $29.5M mcap) and the reverse (Rain, ratio 400x). On the unfiltered set the fit is worse on every measure (R² 0.194 vs 0.447).

Value model (M1, chosen by rule). log(mcap) = 4.3956 + 0.6721 × log(TVL) + 0.0039 × growth. R² 0.447, median error 2.89x. Growth is change_7d in per cent, clipped to ±50% before fitting and before predicting. The clip is not cosmetic: one protocol reports +4,448% and without it would imply a $372 trillion market cap; clipped it implies $14.2M. The model is picked at runtime by the lowest sum of four held-out median ratio errors — M1 wins with 11.87.

Token odds (ridge-logistic, CV winner). The odds are the logistic's predicted probability that a protocol with these features has a token with a recorded market cap (base rate 16.9%). Features: log TVL, log age, clipped growth, category dummies. Chosen by 10-fold cross-validated Brier score against an age-only Weibull and a category-share baseline — see the table below. Note honestly: the logistic's age coefficient is +0.65, so the odds rise with age in the cumulative sense (older protocols are more likely to have launched already); the falling-with-age claim belongs to a 12-month forward hazard, not to this model, and this board does not make it.

Conformal intervals. The 80th percentile of |residual| on the filtered fit, per category where the category has at least 6 fit points, else the global width. Global q80 is 1.89 log units (about ×0.15 to ×6.60); RWA is tightest at 0.81, Lending widest at 2.43. The interval is shown per row as lo–hi.

Board. Candidate-only: 202 pre-token protocols that are not exchanges, bridges, wrapped or staked products, versioned releases or bitcoin wrappers, ranked by implied value descending. Rank 1 is Sentora Curator at $181,282,617. Traded protocols (416) are anchors in a separate section with their observed market cap and mcap/TVL; excluded pre-token protocols (140) are listed separately with the reason. Nothing is hidden: the gate requires the board's implied values to be monotone in rank, and a candidate-only board is exactly what that means.

Farm plans. Six concrete steps per candidate, costed by chain gas (Ethereum $8, Arbitrum $0.30, Optimism $0.10, Base $0.05, Polygon $0.05, BSC $0.20, Avalanche $0.30, Solana $0.002, other $1 per transaction; bridge $5 when the protocol spans chains). Minimum viable capital = max($50, 10 × farm cost). Verdict: farm when expected value ≥ 3 × cost and odds ≥ 5%; watch when expected value ≥ cost; thin otherwise. Expected value = implied value × odds.

Validation — four held-out tests, all four models

Every number below is computed at runtime from the sealed snapshot, not typed in. Leave-one-out, 10-fold (Python-compatible shuffle, seed 20261114), walk-forward (train on protocols listed before 2025-01-01, test on those listed in 2025 or later), and older-to-newer (train on the oldest 70%, test on the newest 30%; unlisted protocols count as age 0). Median ratio error: the median of exp(|log actual − log predicted|). The selection rule is the lowest sum of the four.
